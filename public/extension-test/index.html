How to read the result

Hidden or unfocused samples mean those signals were observable. If it reports visible and focused throughout while you were away, that is a mismatch with your action, not proof that an extension is undetectable.

Recording starts on load. Log keeps the latest 300 events. Pointer movement is sampled at most once per second.
